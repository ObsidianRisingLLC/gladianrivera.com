Why I Keep Learning

I have never been satisfied with knowing just enough. Curiosity is what gets me into the room. Discipline is what keeps me learning once I’m there.

Across public service, organizational leadership, and now entrepreneurship, I have deliberately studied the systems I work inside: federal operations, criminal justice, victim services, trauma-informed practice, executive leadership, organizational strategy, and, most recently, artificial intelligence.

Each season of learning gave me a new lens. How institutions actually function. How trauma shapes the people moving through them. How teams grow, resist, and change. And how technology can strengthen the human side of the work instead of replacing it.

Beyond the Classroom

Training gave me language and frameworks. The real education came from putting them to work: co-creating a federal reentry court program, building operational infrastructure, leading initiatives through complexity, and founding Obsidian Rising LLC to help organizations adopt AI responsibly.

Every course I complete is only as valuable as what I do with it. That’s the standard I hold myself to.

Mastery is not a destination. It is the daily practice of becoming more capable, more informed, and more effective in the work we choose to do.
