The only information collected directly through this site is what you choose to submit through the contact form: your name, email address, and message. This site does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels.

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry. Information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, or as required by the third-party services above.

Your Rights

You may request that any information you've submitted be deleted or corrected at any time by reaching out through the contact page.

Children's Privacy

Changes to This Policy

Questions
