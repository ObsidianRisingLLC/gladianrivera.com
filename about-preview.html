The Story

Gladian Rivera is a strategist, entrepreneur, author, speaker, and creator whose work sits at the intersection of leadership, systems, technology, justice, and human transformation.

For more than a decade, her career has taken her through nonprofit organizations, state government, healthcare, and the federal justice systems. Her work includes designing programs, developing operational infrastructure, improving processes, leading initiatives, and helping turn ideas into practical systems that leaders could actually use.

While working in the federal justice system, she helped co-create a reentry court initiative, work that contributed to receiving a 2024 EOUSA Director’s Award.

Today, she brings that same systems-thinking approach into emerging technology. As the founder of Obsidian Rising LLC, she helps small businesses implement AI responsibly, and build structures that support both people and performance.

She is also the author of The Sovereign Leader: Leading from Inner Authority After the Forge. Her book explores what happens when experience, adversity, discernment, and self-knowledge reshape the way we lead.

Currently, she is continuing those conversations through The Gladian Rivera Show, a podcast. The podcast holds candid conversations and personal reflections on leadership, mental wellness, entrepreneurship, technology, creativity, and the experiences that shape who we become. And the strange, fascinating experience of being human in a world changing faster than most of us can fully process.

She is fascinated by the places where seemingly different worlds collide: technology and humanity, logic and intuition, and leadership and identity.
